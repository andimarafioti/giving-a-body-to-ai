The quirks

What a robot must do
while you talk

01

Look at you

Find the speaker and turn toward them.

02

Show it listens

Small movements while you speak.

03

Know you are done

A pause is not always the end of a turn.

04

Let you interrupt

Stop talking and moving the moment you cut in.

05

Answer fast

Silence feels longer when a face looks at you.

06

Move while talking

Otherwise it is a speaker on a stand.
