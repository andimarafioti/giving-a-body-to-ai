Giving a body to AI: live deck
==============================

Online: https://andimarafioti.github.io/giving-a-body-to-ai/ (Chrome; press F for fullscreen).

Offline, from a copy of this repository (no network needed):

    python3 serve.py

then open http://localhost:8765/index.html in Chrome, and press F for fullscreen.
Opening index.html straight from disk does not work: the deck fetches its slides and 3D models over HTTP.

Keys
    Right, Space, Page Down   next slide or step (clickers send these)
    Left, Page Up             back
    N                         speaker notes in a second window (allow the pop-up; drag it to your laptop screen)
    K                         keynote mode on/off: skips the slides marked "Keynote: cut" in the notes
    F                         fullscreen
    Home / End                first / last slide

#12 in the address bar jumps to slide 12, #8.2 to step 2 of slide 8.

The robot is drawn live by the laptop's graphics chip. Any recent laptop is fine; plug it in, so the
browser doesn't throttle the GPU on battery.

3D scene, robot models and slide design come from Fabien Danieau's Reachy Mini deck
(github.com/FabienDanieau/reachy_mini_intro, CC BY 4.0); see README.md for the model licences.
